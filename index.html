It started as a dream

When Laura was a little girl she had the same dream four or five times a year, for a few years. She gave it a name.

"I even named it, as you can probably guess, The Witch in the Envelope."

The book is a more grown up version of that dream, but the heart of it stayed.

  1. Oct to Dec 2016Planned and outlined the whole book.
  2. Mid February 2017Started writing, scared and doing it anyway.
  3. April 2017Finished the zero draft, around 90,000 words.
  4. NowA B.R.A.G. Medallion honoree, three books, and a fourth on Kickstarter.

About Laura

Laura used to teach social sciences and dance to high schoolers, then spent years scanning hearts as an echocardiographer. Now she writes full time from what she calls a little paradise in northeast Florida, with her college sweetheart and their two girls.

Away from the page she walks, crochets, leads worship, does jigsaw puzzles and plays a lot of board games. She also puts a content rating on every book, so readers know what they are opening before they open it.
